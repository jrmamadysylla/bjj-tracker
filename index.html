Daily mobility 15 min
Mat, kettlebell and belt only. No bar needed. Based on BJJ-specific research — hip CARs, 90/90, frog stretch and thoracic work are the highest-impact exercises for grapplers.
Today's routine
Hips / BJJ
Hip CARs
On all fours, brace core. Lift one knee and slowly circle through full range — forward, out, back, down. 5 slow reps each direction, each side. Trains active hip control for guard retention and escapes.
Hips / desk job
90/90 hip stretch
Front and back leg at 90°. Lean forward over front shin, hold 45 sec each side × 2. Most important stretch for guard depth and desk hip flexors.
Hips / BJJ
Frog stretch
On all fours, slide knees wide. Sink hips back slowly, hold 45 sec × 2. Increases hip external rotation — directly relevant to side control, guard and passing.
Hips / BJJ + KB
Cossack squat
Wide stance, shift into one leg, sink into deep lateral lunge. 8 slow reps each side. Hold KB at chest for depth. Best single exercise for BJJ hip mobility.
Thoracic / desk + BJJ
Thread the needle
On all fours, slide one arm under body and rotate open. Hold 30 sec each side × 2. Undoes desk and BJJ top position hunching. Opens shoulder internal rotation.
Thoracic / BJJ
Thoracic rotation (seated)
Sit cross-legged, hands behind head. Rotate slowly each direction, 10 reps × 2. Keeps your pressure driving down into your opponent.
Hamstrings / belt
Lying hamstring stretch
Lie on back, loop BJJ belt around one foot, pull leg toward you. Hold 45 sec each side. Essential for guard play and hip escapes when stacked.
Core / lifting
Dead bug
Lie on back, arms up, knees at 90°. Lower opposite arm and leg without arching lower back. 8 slow reps each side. Anti-extension core work.
Ankles + hips
Ankle circles + squat hold
10 slow ankle circles each direction. Then deep squat hold, heels down, elbows pushing knees out, 60 sec. Ankle prep and hip depth combined.
Shoulders / KB
KB arm bar or shoulder CARs
With KB: lie on back, press KB up, rotate open 30 sec each side. Without KB: slow shoulder circles, 5 each direction per side. Most abused joint in grappling.
Wrists / lifting + BJJ
Wrist prep circuit
Circles, extension holds on floor, prayer and reverse prayer. 60 sec total. Protects for bench, pressing and BJJ gripping.
Neck / desk job
Neck release + chin tucks
Ear-to-shoulder hold 20 sec each side. Then chin tucks 10 reps. Counters forward head posture from screens and BJJ pressure passing.
