Daily mobility 15 min
Built for your stack: desk job (hip flexors, thoracic spine, neck) + BJJ (hip rotation, shoulders, hamstrings) + lifting (ankles, lats, wrists). Do this every morning before training or as a standalone on rest days.
Today's routine
Desk / hips
90/90 hip stretch
Front and back leg at 90°. Lean forward over front shin. 45 sec each side × 2. Opens guard depth and counters sitting.
Desk / thoracic
Thoracic rotation
Sit cross-legged, hands behind head, rotate slowly each side. 10 reps × 2. Undoes desk and BJJ top position hunching.
BJJ / shoulders
Thread the needle
On all fours, slide arm under body and rotate. Hold 30 sec each side × 2. Opens shoulder internal rotation for escapes.
BJJ / hips
Deep squat hold
Heels down, elbows pushing knees out. Hold 60 sec total. Builds guard depth and hip flexor length under load.
Lifting / ankles
Ankle circles + wall drill
10 slow circles each direction, then knee-to-wall drill 10 reps per side. Essential for squat depth.
Lifting / lats
Lat stretch on bar
One hand on pull-up bar, let hip drop to same side. Hold 30 sec each side × 2. Improves overhead range and squat bar position.
Lifting / wrists
Wrist prep circuit
Circles, extension holds on floor, prayer and reverse prayer. 60 sec total. Protects for bench, front rack, and BJJ gripping.
Desk / neck
Neck release + chin tucks
Ear-to-shoulder 20 sec each side. Then chin tucks 10 reps. Counters forward head posture from screens and BJJ pressure.
